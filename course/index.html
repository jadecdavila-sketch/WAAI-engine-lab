The High Level

Key elements in the framework that keep the engine running smoothly.

One framework

The Lab builds the products WAAI needs and teaches members by having them build. Counterpoint is the framework for both: the same Council, the same Journey, the same checks, whether the work is a three week experiment or a product members will use.

A human checks every piece of work

Every piece of work carries a check a human performs before it is accepted. Nothing ships to members until a release checklist is signed. This, accompanied by the framework, is the Lab's answer to 'AI slop'.

3-5 hours a week

One critique session a week, everything recorded, a showcase at every gate instead of a competition. Built for members with jobs, families, and businesses.

Teams of two to four

Projects are split into small teams formed at onboarding. Experiments are welcome. One weekly review runs through the Council lenses, and anything launched to members runs the whole framework.

The Counterpoint framework

A ten stage journey takes products from 'good' to 'great.' Three parts govern every stage: the North Star, the Council, and the Foundation.

The Journey

Ten stages from Discovery to Scale. Each has a question, a definition of done, and the Council voices that lead it. A gate at the end of each stage is a Council review held to the North Star. Inside the stages sit eighty nine activities, each naming who drives, what AI can carry, what a human must own, and how the human knows it was done right.

Module 3 walks all ten.

The Product North Star

The product thesis: who it is for, what it must do, and what design moves are off limits, written specifically enough that even an AI agent can tell when a decision violates it.

No project opens a code editor without one.

The Council

Nine voices, each reading the work through their own unique lens. Members are the Council builders for WAAI products, and AI critics run the same lenses continuously, in the Lab and, through the Counterpoint Council plugin for Claude, in any member's own workspace. The Council surfaces tensions rather than voting on them.

Critique is structured, expected, and safe.

The Foundation

Living documentation every person and every agent reads before touching the codebase and updates before leaving it. A pre push hook makes the workflow binding. Drawn here as a road atlas of one real codebase, Larkin's. Hover or tap any marker.

The handoff document is always current.

The one rule you will meet in every module

A human is responsible for every activity, in every mode. Whoever drives, a person confirms the work passed its check before it is accepted.

Counterpoint is named for the musical idea: independent voices held together by rules, so that the tension between them makes something alive. The nine voices of the Council are the clearest case, and the same idea runs through the stages, the checks, and the documentation.

This course covers the Council, the North Star (with Counterpoint's own as the example), and the Journey. The Foundation gets its own session, when the Counterpoint plugin arrives and you can watch it run. A preview of it is here, drawn as a road atlas.

How this course works

The modules are meant to be taken in order, but every one is open, so you can move between them freely. The line at the top of every page shows where you are and takes you to any module. Your answers and your notes are saved in this browser only, so you can leave and come back.

When you are ready, take the quiz. A passing score gives you a completion code to post in the Lab channel, and that is what gets you a seat in session two.
