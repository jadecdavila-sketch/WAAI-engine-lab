The short version

One framework

Using the same framework and tooling for building out WAAI products allows for flexibility for its Builders, and for unity across products and tooling.

Regular human checks and reviews

Every piece of work has a check, and a person runs it before the work is accepted. Nothing ships to members until the release checklist is signed.

Three to five hours a week

To engage with a project, we ask for 3-5 hours a week of your time. This enables us to keep momentum on very needed products and tools, and allows time for group review.

Build pods

The Lab will build WAAI infrastructure in small pods, and we'll review the work together on a weekly basis. This will enable us to build out the tooling in parallel.

The Counterpoint framework

Ten stages take a product from an idea to something people rely on and love to use.

The Journey

Ten stages from Discovery to Scale. Every stage has a question, a definition of done, and Council voices that lead it. Inside the stages sit eighty nine activities, each naming who drives, what AI can carry, what a person must own, and how you know it was done right.

The Product North Star

Who the product is for, what it must do, and what it will not do, written plainly enough that an AI agent can tell when a decision breaks it.

No project opens a code editor without one.

The Council

Nine voices, each reading the work through one lens. In the Lab, members sit in those seats. AI critics run the same lenses the rest of the time, in the Lab and, through the Counterpoint plugin, on your own computer. The Council does not vote. It names the tensions and hands them to you.

Critique is structured, expected, and safe.

The Foundation

A living map of the codebase. Every person and every agent reads it before touching the code and updates it before leaving. A hook on the way out makes that binding.

The handoff document is always current.

The one rule you will meet in every module

A human is responsible for every activity, in every mode. Whoever drives, a person confirms the work passed its check before it is accepted.

Counterpoint is named for the musical idea: independent voices held together by rules, so that the tension between them makes something alive. Think: Pachelbel's Canon in D.

How this course works

Best in order, but every module is open, so go where you like. The line at the top of each page shows where you are and takes you anywhere. Your answers are saved in this browser only, so you can leave and come back. Clear the browser and they go with it.

When you are ready, take the quiz. Twelve of fifteen gives you a completion code. Post it in the Lab channel and your seat in session two is held.
