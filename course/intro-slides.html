Session two · Discovery

Three ideas before we open the tool.

Counterpoint is about to ask you about jobs and objects. Ten minutes on what those words mean, and where pain fits in, will make the rest of today much easier.

Today's practice project

Meet Ama and her savings circle.

Ama runs a savings circle with eight friends. Every month each woman pays in 50, and one of them takes home the whole pot. Some call it a susu, some a daret, some a tanda.

Today

A notebook and a group chat. Every month someone asks "did I pay yet?" or "when is my turn?". Last spring two women both thought June was theirs.

What she wants

One simple place that keeps the record straight, so nobody has to take her word for it.

The one big limit

It only keeps the record. It never holds or moves money.

The brief

What Ama needs, in short.

The moments that matter
  • First of the month: who has paid and who has not?
  • A member hands over her money and Ama records it on the spot.
  • A member checks, on her own, what she has paid and when her turn comes.
  • Payout day: the pot went to the right person.
  • Someone pays late, noted without making it awkward.
The people
  • Ama, the organizer. Trusted, busy, on her phone between other things.
  • A member. Wants to see her own record and her turn, and to know the circle is run fairly.
The limits
  • It only keeps the record. It never holds or moves money.
  • One circle, about eight to twelve members.
  • Easy on a phone, for women who are not all comfortable with technology.
  • Members see their own payments and the turn order. Only Ama records.
Not in it
  • No bank connections, no payments in the app, no interest or loans.
  • No sharing outside the circle.
  • One circle per organizer for now.

Idea one · Pain

Pain shows you where to look.

1

Name the pain, briefly

Every month Ama answers the same two questions for eight women. One sentence is enough.

2

Ask what she is really after

Not "a faster way to answer". She wants everyone to trust the record.

3

Picture the day with the pain gone

Each member can see her own payments and her turn. The question never gets asked.

The trap

Copying the old way onto a screen. A notebook Ama still has to fill in by hand is the same pain with a battery.

Idea two · Jobs to be done

People hire a product to get something done.

"People don't want a quarter-inch drill. They want a quarter-inch hole."

  • Nobody wants an app.They want the progress the app gives them. That old marketing line is the whole idea.
  • A job is that progress, from her side.It names a moment, what she wants in that moment, and what it gets her.

Notice

No screens, no buttons, no dashboard. A feature is one answer. The job is the question it answers.

Jobs to be done

Why jobs come first when AI is building.

AI builds whatever you describe, gaps and all. Jobs are how you describe the right thing.

  • 1They tell you what to leave out.If a feature serves no job, it does not go in.
  • 2They stay true when the design changes.The screens may be redrawn ten times. Ama still needs to know who has paid.
  • 3They give you a way to check the work.You cannot read the code, but you can ask "can Ama do this job now?"

Idea three · Objects

Find the things before the screens.

Objects are the things your people already talk about. Listen to Ama and you hear them: the circle, a member, a payment, a turn, the pot. Designers call this object-oriented UX, or OOUX.

What it holds

A contribution holds an amount, a month, and who paid it.

How it connects

A contribution belongs to one member and one circle.

What you can do with it

Record it, mark it late, look it up.

Counterpoint asks these same three questions about every object.

Objects

The power is in how they connect.

A first guess. Yours may come out different, and that is fine.

  • One name for one thing.If one screen says "payment" and another says "contribution", people get lost, and so does the AI.
  • Objects become the data.Get them wrong now and every screen built on top is wrong too.
  • You can see a change ripple.Add "late fee" and the map shows what it touches.
