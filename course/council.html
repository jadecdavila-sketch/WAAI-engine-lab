The Council

Nine voices, each reading the work through one lens. In a review, members are the voices. AI critics run the same lenses the rest of the time. The Council does not vote. It names the tensions and hands them to the team.

About an hourNine voices, each with a five question check

How a review works

The work goes in front of the Council at every gate and at the weekly critique. Each voice reads it through its own lens and says what it sees. Where two voices disagree, nobody smooths it over. The disagreement is written down as a tension: a decision the team now makes on purpose, with the cost each way.

Every voice is also a seat on a project team and a guild across the Lab. The Lab intake asks where you stand on each of the nine. The ones you call yours are the lens you bring to every review you sit in.

Open any voice below. Its page carries the voice in its own words, where it leads on the Journey, and every member of its council in full: core wisdom, war stories, toolkit, allies and tensions. Each page ends with a five question check.
