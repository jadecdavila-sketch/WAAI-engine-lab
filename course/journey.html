How to read the stages

Every stage is made of activities

Open any stage above and you will see its work broken into activities from the catalog. Each one carries a mode that says who drives, a set of suggested tools, and a human-confirmed check before the work is accepted.

The four modes

  • human-onlyAI does not take part in the core act. Interviewing a user. Watching someone use the product. Making the call.
  • human-leadsThe human does the work, with AI helping on drafts, options, and drudgery.
  • human-ai-collaborateReal back and forth. Neither could do it as well alone.
  • ai-leadsAI does the work end to end. The human writes the spec, reviews the output, and verifies it against the check before accepting it.

A human is responsible for every activity in every mode. Whoever drives, the human still has to confirm the work passed its check.

At the end of the Journey: the release checklist

Nothing ships to members until every item is true and the Lab Lead has signed.

  1. The North Star exists, is current, and the product is recognizably itself against it
  2. Every stage gate was held and its tensions are recorded
  3. The Foundation is current and the pre push hook has never been bypassed
  4. Tests and evals pass, and a deliberately broken release was blocked at least once
  5. Accessibility audit complete; an assistive technology user completed the core task
  6. Privacy review signed by whoever holds that responsibility for WAAI
  7. AI surfaces red teamed, with the attack list and fixes recorded
  8. A beta with real members happened, and at least one thing changed because of it
  9. Monitoring, alerting, and a runbook exist, and a named person receives the alerts
  10. A maintainer is named for after launch, and a succession plan exists
