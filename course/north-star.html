The Product North Star

The page every agent reads before it works, and the lens every Council review looks through. Here you read Counterpoint's own and test it the way the Council does. Yours gets written in session two, inside the plugin, in your own words.

30 minutesOne exercise, on your own

What a North Star contains

Who it is for
One person, in one season of her life, and what she is carrying. Written the way you would describe her to a friend.
The thesis
What this makes possible for her that was not possible before. Two to four sentences. The line every Council report ends with.
Principles
Three to five. Each is a consequence of the thesis, with a test a reviewer can run in one breath. A principle with no test is a slogan.
What good looks like
Contrast pairs. What any product would say on the left, what seeing her sounds like on the right.
Anti patterns
What the product will not do, however sound the engineering. Starts short and grows from Council runs.
A test for anything
Three or four questions every voice runs before it speaks.
The tenth voice
The nine Council voices are the same on every project. The tenth is her, seated from the who section, speaking first person.

Precedence. When technical correctness and this document disagree, this document wins. When a generic heuristic disagrees with a principle here, the principle wins.

Counterpoint's own

The framework you are learning has a North Star too, and this is it: written in Discovery, with the Lab's Movement woven in. Read the who and the thesis slowly. Everything below them was proposed from those two sections.

How three voices read it

Three voices read the who and the thesis. Each one gives a read, names its concerns against a section, and makes one ask. This is what a Council does to a document. These asks are still open, on purpose.

You can now

  • Say what a North Star contains and what it is for
  • Tell a principle from a slogan, and spot where a North Star has a gap
  • Say why the who and the thesis come first, and what gets proposed from them
